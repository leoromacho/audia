Audia Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026.

Audia is a text-to-speech reading app developed by Leopoldo Romacho. This page explains what data the app collects and why.

What data is collected

With your explicit consent (asked for the first time you open the app, and which you can withdraw at any time from Settings → Privacy), Audia uses Firebase Analytics / Google Analytics to understand how the app is used:

  • Which screens you visit and how much time you spend on each one.
  • Which buttons and features you use (for laying, searching, purchasing).
  • An anonymous app-install identifier generated by Firebase, which does not identify a person.

Audia does not collect your name, email, or any personally identifiable data. Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used either: the app o never access it, so no App Tracking Transparencypermission prompt is shown when opening the app.

Who it's shared with

Data is processed by Google Ireland Limiteds / Google Analytics, acting as data processor. Itis not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes, and it is not shared with any other service.

Legal basis and consent

Processing is based on your explicit consenan grant or withdraw it at any time fromSettings → Privacy → "Share usage data" inside the app. Withdrawing it does not affect how Audia works: the app functions the same with or without ana

How long data is kept

Event data is retained according to Google Analytics 4's standard settings, after which it is automatically deleted.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rection, and portability by writing to audia.app.support@gmail.com. You may also file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, you'll be notified inside the app.
